BLANCO TV - WINDOWS 10/11 - OPTIONAL DISPLAY 1.4

1. Extract ALL files, then double-click Start-Blanco.cmd.
2. Put PC and TV on the same LAN. Enable ADB debugging on the TV.
3. Enter TV IP and accept authorization ON THE TV.
   Wireless debugging users also enter its separate pairing port and code.
4. The first inspection ONLY READS the model, Android version and current Home.
   Installation is OFF by default. Answer y to install/open the optional app.
5. The tool installs/updates Blanco and opens it. It NEVER changes default Home,
   sets Device Owner, disables apps, removes accounts or factory-resets anything.
6. Scan the on-screen QR, sign in to Blanco administration and choose an exit PIN.
7. Press Back SEVEN times in FIVE seconds, enter the PIN and return to Fire TV.
   Select Administration on the PIN dialog if you want settings instead.
   The same seven-tap gesture works on the top-left corner. Home is not locked.
8. Blanco requests reopening on screen wake and while the active display session
   is in the background (at most once every 30 seconds). A correct PIN exit is
   persisted and stops reopening until manual launch or the next full boot if
   auto-start is enabled. TV sleep, revocation and owner maintenance block retries.
   No launch success is guaranteed: Fire TV may block background activities or
   kill the service. Keep the original launcher. No overlay/admin grants requested.
9. Disconnect using the installer and disable network debugging after testing.

Android 8+ with System WebView required. Fire OS 7 is based on Android 9;
actual TV/Windows compatibility still requires a physical test. No guarantee
is made against every device failure. Keep the original Fire TV launcher.
No signing-key mismatch is fixed by uninstalling the old app automatically.
If an OLDER installer already enabled device management or changed Home,
this tool does not silently reverse that configuration: report it first.
Backups: %LOCALAPPDATA%\BlancoTV\Backups
ADB is Google's unmodified Platform-Tools; accompanying licenses are retained.
