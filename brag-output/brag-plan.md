# Brag Plan — Blanco, Grenoble

## Nine-question planning rubric
1. App: Blanco's French café menu and customer loyalty card in Grenoble.
2. Strongest promise from product copy: « Votre fidélité récompensée. »
3. Visual hook: airborne coffee and iced-matcha cups spilling sculptural liquid, requested by the owner.
4. Actual product: public French menu and signup screenshots; Syrian coffee/tea and Halloumi/Labneh/Hoummous names transcribed from the published menu.
5. Minimum satisfying film: 22 seconds, 1080 × 1920, 30 fps.
6. Tone: polished, with playful physical product movement; mint café editorial identity.
7. Audio: warm restrained bed, Sascha Ende vol.12, and three low-risk reveal/click accents. No narration.
8. Caption: « Blanco, votre pause café à Grenoble. Syrian coffee, Syrian tea, matcha et petites faims : découvrez notre carte et rejoignez le Club Blanco. »
9. User flow: discover the menu → scan the loyalty QR → create a loyalty card. No real customer account is filmed.

## Angle and source boundaries
Customer-facing French launch, not an engineering demo. Food and beverages lead; loyalty is a short payoff.
Owner asked for Syrian, Turkish and Lebanese influences. Published menu currently confirms Syrian coffee, Syrian tea, Halloumi, Labneh and Hoummous. No named dessert or Turkish-specific item was found; do not invent baklava, knafeh, availability, prices, opening offers or address.
Generated cups are illustrative advertising art, not photographs of the café's actual takeaway packaging. Existing sandwich imagery is a temporary illustrative asset already used by the app. No customer PII, login codes, private links, account screenshots or secrets.

## Visual identity / LOOK
LOOK: #f7f6f2 paper, #afd5b6 mint, #1c211e ink; Roca One display 76–120px and Montserrat body 30–40px from bundled project fonts; 80px safe margins; 28px rules; food occupies 55–65% of the canvas. Motion: 0.65–1.0s long-tail arrivals, 12–18° cup rotation, slow 25px float, 0.45s exits.
BANNED: developer dashboards, fictitious products, blue SaaS gradients, equalizer graphics, illegible flashes, customer personal data, invented reward targets.

## Storyboard (22 seconds total)
1. 0–5.5s — « Votre pause. / Un peu plus Blanco. » Coffee and matcha cups fly in on separate arcs; droplets already captured in generated splash art. Both settle, tilt gently, then exit. Label « CAFÉ · MATCHA »; Grenoble appears small but readable.
2. 5.5–11s — « Des saveurs à partager. » Real names: « Syrian coffee · Syrian tea » and « Halloumi · Labneh · Hoummous ». Sandwich image and real menu screenshot make the regional focus concrete. Names enter in two steps with at least 2.4s settled reading time. No unsupported dessert claim.
3. 11–16.5s — « Votre fidélité récompensée. » Actual public signup screen in a phone frame beside real loyalty QR. « Scannez. Créez votre carte. » Simulated short tap/scan accent; no submission or fictitious real balance.
4. 16.5–22s — Blanco logo lockup and flying cups return. « On se retrouve chez Blanco. » « Grenoble · Coffee & More ». Public menu QR holds 4s. Music fades over last 1.8s. Tiny credit stays readable: « Musique : Sascha Ende · ende.app · CC BY 4.0 ».

## Music cue guidance
Read bundled vol.12 cue preset. Major reveal near 10.93s; outro landing near 17.47s. Reading time takes priority over cue locks. Music gain 0.30; 0.4s fade-in, 1.8s fade-out; sparse SFX 0.4–0.5. Audio-reactive modulation omitted: physical food movement is the priority, no visualizer.
